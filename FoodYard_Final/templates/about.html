ABOUT FOODYARD

A simple system for a meaningful problem.

FoodYard is a college project that demonstrates how a web application can organize food donations, donor accounts and administrative tracking in one place.

THE IDEA

Reduce waste. Improve coordination.

Restaurants, households, events and other donors can have safe surplus food. FoodYard provides a structured way to record that food and manage its progress.

The system keeps donor and administrator responsibilities separate, making the workflow easy to understand and safer to operate.
